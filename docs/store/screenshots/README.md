# Identity candidate captures

These actual emulator captures were produced during the interrupted Opus design pass and retained for design review. They are not certified screenshots of the final rebuilt PBW: cached emulator binaries do not byte-match the final build. They contain simulated home state, not a live Telegram account.

Avery visually reviewed the emery, chalk and diorite captures. Fresh final-build installation failed on emery and timed out on chalk. No gabbro runtime capture is available; docs/identity/preview-gabbro.png is a labelled deterministic illustration, not runtime proof.

Do not upload these as final release screenshots until the candidate is approved and final-build captures or real-device screenshots are obtained. Existing docs/screenshots captures predate this identity refresh.
