# Squire Plus: Store listing draft

Draft copy and asset list for the Rebble Store listing. Nothing here has been uploaded; a

## Name

Squire Plus

## Category

Tools & Utilities

## Short description

Talk to your own AI agent from your wrist, through Telegram.

## Description

Squire Plus puts your own AI agent on your Pebble. Press SELECT, ask a question out loud,
and your agent's answer appears on your watch.

It connects to an agent you already run, such as Hermes or OpenClaw, through that agent's
Telegram bot. Your phone talks to Telegram directly. There is no Squire Plus server in
between.

- Ask by voice, read the reply on your wrist.
- Pick up your last conversation where you left off.
- Recent and sample prompts one press away.
- Optional location, so your agent can answer "what's nearby?". Off until you allow it.
- Build targets: basalt, chalk, diorite, emery and gabbro. Android use is user-tested; iOS is unverified.

**What you need**

- Your own Telegram account. You sign in on the watch or in the app's settings.
- An AI agent bot you've set up yourself in Hermes or OpenClaw. Squire Plus doesn't
  include an agent; it connects you to yours.
- A Pebble phone app with full PebbleKit JS support. Cobble isn't supported.

You don't need Telegram developer credentials to use this app.

**Privacy**

Your prompts, your agent's replies and (only if you allow it) your location travel through
Telegram to the agent bot you choose, and are kept in that Telegram chat and by your agent's
backend. Your Telegram sign-in stays on your phone. Squire Plus has no maintainer-operated relay or analytics server. Your selected bot and
phone dictation service process data according to their own policies.

**About**

Squire Plus is an independently maintained fork of Squire by Justin Sunseri, which is based
on Bobby by Katharine Berry. It is maintained by Andreas Varotsis and is not supported by
the original authors. Unofficial; not affiliated with or endorsed by Telegram.
Open source under Apache 2.0: https://github.com/AndreasThinks/pebble-squire

## Notes for the maintainer before using this copy

- The platform list in the description maps to basalt (Time, Time Steel), chalk (Time
  Round), diorite (Pebble 2), emery (Time 2) and gabbro. Check the marketing name the Store
  uses for gabbro, and add it, before publishing.
- Whether Telegram's API terms permit this kind of client is unresolved. The copy avoids any
  claim of compliance or endorsement; keep it that way.
- The copy claims nothing about iOS. Android Store installs and core flows have user-reported
  acceptance (see [RELEASE-0.1.0.md](RELEASE-0.1.0.md)); iOS is unverified.
