# Squire Plus v0.1

An independently maintained fork of Squire by Justin Sunseri, based on Bobby by Katharine Berry.

## What is new
A refreshed watch interface, clearer onboarding and sign-in, improved conversation history, and more reliable message delivery.

## Setup
Sign in to Telegram and choose your own Hermes or OpenClaw agent bot. Squire Plus is a separate app and does not reuse the original Squire login or settings.
